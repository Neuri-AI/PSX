**Numeric policy:** all used geometry resides on the signed `1/64px` `LayoutUnit` grid; additions/subtractions and repeated gap accumulation saturate raw `i32`. Integer multiplications widen to `i64/i128` before the clamp. Finite inputs whose CSS percentage products overflow `f64` are clamped to the signed raw endpoint. Pure resolved `f64` factor/weight arithmetic has no `LayoutUnit` storage; if an intermediate becomes nonfinite, computation **fails explicitly and consistently**, not by inventing a rectangle or propagating NaN. Distinct rounding operations remain documented rather than imposing post-hoc rectangle quantization.
